ParkShare – Event Parking Marketplace Web App (Map, Booking, Host Dashboard)

## Your role
Product designer & full-stack front-end developer

## Project description
Parking near stadiums, concert venues and downtowns is expensive and sells out fast, while driveways, church lots and business lots a short walk away sit empty. ParkShare is a two-sided marketplace demo that lets those neighbors rent their spaces to drivers on game day, by the hour or by the month.

**What I built**
- **Driver app:** venue/city search, interactive map with price pins, filters (event/hourly/monthly, price, EV, oversize, tailgating), spot detail pages, transparent price breakdown, mock checkout, and a mobile parking pass with QR code, directions and add-to-calendar.
- **Host app:** an 8-step "List your spot" wizard with automatic event detection and price suggestions from nearby listings, a required permission check (HOA/lease/city rules), and a dashboard with earnings chart, payouts and driver check-in by code or license plate.
- **Trust & safety:** verified-host badges, per-city local-rules banners, reviews, cancellation policies with automatic refunds, and report-a-problem flows.
- **Research-backed:** the product was shaped by market, competitor, legal and business-model research (pricing, fees, city regulations, insurance) that I ran before building.

**Results:** fully clickable on desktop and mobile, 8 venues and 40 sample listings, and 36 automated browser tests passing with zero console errors.
